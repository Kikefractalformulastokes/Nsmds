# Materials — Claims Boundary

**Permitted:** "first internal scientific vertical," "E2 internal," "ready for independent
reproduction."

**Blocked:** "E3," "validated externally," "certified," "independently validated" (until an
actual third party completes reproduction, per `MATERIALS_REPRODUCTION_PROTOCOL.md`).

Same discipline as `research/paper-01-dual-observable/PUBLICATION_GATE.md` in `nsmds` — that
paper is a good model: it explicitly gates every claim behind unmet conditions rather than
asserting them early.
