# SEM Master Plan

**Hard rule (per brief and enforced here): no paid campaign launches until conversion tracking
is live and verified.** No ad account is connected to this session — nothing in this section can
or does spend money.

## Phasing

- **Phase 1 — RiesgoDeObra** (direct commercial intent): revisión presupuesto obra, auditoría
  obra, defectos constructivos, reclamación constructora, informe técnico obra, perito obra.
- **Phase 2 — ModelAssuranceLab / CoreSyn enterprise**: only once a landing page and tracking
  exist.
- **Phase 3 — Aerospace / Materials**: no mass ads — ABM/outreach, LinkedIn, technical content
  instead (these are long-cycle, low-volume, high-value relationships, not SEM-suited).
