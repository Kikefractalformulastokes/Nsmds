# SEM Budget Rules

1. No spend without conversion tracking verified live and firing correctly.
2. Start with a small test budget (Enrique sets the number — not fabricated here) for the first
   1-2 weeks, per campaign.
3. Kill any ad group with zero leads after a defined spend/click threshold (Enrique sets the
   number based on actual CPCs once observed — not guessed here).
4. Optimize for qualified leads, not clicks — CPL and lead quality are the north-star metrics,
   not CTR.
5. Track lead → call/brief → payment conversion, not just form fills.
6. Weekly budget review against `AZURE_COST_MONITORING.md`-style tracking (separate ledger for
   ad spend vs. Azure spend — don't mix the two).
7. No campaign launch without Enrique's explicit approval, every time — not a standing blanket
   approval.
