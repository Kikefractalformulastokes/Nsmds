# Publication gate — Paper 01

## Current classification

- Public repository draft: allowed.
- Technical discussion and protocol review: allowed.
- Academic preprint/DOI: blocked.
- Claim of independent validation: blocked.

## Required before archival preprint release

1. Recover or reconstruct the complete executable package.
2. Freeze the baseline, candidate configurations and acceptance thresholds before the final rerun.
3. Record dataset provenance, licence notes and SHA-256 checksums.
4. Record environment, dependencies, random seeds, mesh/configuration identifiers and compute budget.
5. Run the package from zero in a clean internal environment.
6. Regenerate every table and figure from retained raw outputs.
7. Verify that pressure-distribution improvement does not violate the lift-polar gate.
8. Include negative, null and sensitivity results.
9. Complete an IP/disclosure review of every equation, parameter and artefact.
10. Obtain author approval of title, affiliation, licence and final manuscript hash.

## Required before an independent-validation claim

1. A third party accepts the frozen protocol and discloses conflicts/compensation.
2. The reviewer receives immutable inputs and hashes before execution.
3. The reviewer reproduces the baseline independently.
4. The reviewer executes the candidate bundle without outcome-dependent assistance.
5. The reviewer returns environment metadata, logs, outputs, hashes and signed conclusions.

A protocol review, email reply or technical discussion is not independent validation.
