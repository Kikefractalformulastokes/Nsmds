# NDT-001 — M10 EVIDENCE PACK

Date: 2026-08-27
Purpose: auditable technical evidence index for the CoreSyn NDT model-assurance case study.

## 1. Scope

Public ultrasonic guided-wave localization benchmark. The work evaluates frozen baselines, generic continuous-localization challengers, noise/OOD behavior, statistical stability, attribution and failure modes. This pack is evidence for an assurance workflow; it is not aircraft certification and does not establish canonical NS-MDS performance.

## 2. Frozen experimental chain

- M4: public KU/Bristol dataset acquisition and baseline reproduction.
- Lift-preserving exploratory challenger: no improvement over 15.00 mm discrete baseline.
- M5: generic continuous localization. KNN2 inverse-square achieved 8.667762 mm MAE on the frozen noise-free holdout; ID10 result 4.637044 mm; OOD KNN2 result 19.575374 mm; bracket OOD 7.086574 mm.
- M5B/M5C: adaptive routing. Noise-free and ID performance retained; OOD 16.226148 mm. Training-only calibration did not solve the real OOD shift.
- M6: paired statistical validation. Observed holdout MAE reduction 42.21%; 36/40 cases better; paired bootstrap CI95% of improvement crossed zero; tail failures remain material.
- M7: ablation/attribution. NN 15 mm; KNN2 uniform 10 mm; KNN2 1/d 9.287 mm; KNN2 1/d^2 8.668 mm; KNN3 degraded strongly. Improvement attributed to generic two-neighbour continuous interpolation, not canonical NS-MDS.
- M8: internal blind re-split. Nearest-neighbour 37.50 mm MAE; frozen challenger 27.93 mm; observed reduction 25.51%; 13/16 cases better. Internal blind validation only.
- M9: claim gate freezes approved/prohibited language.

## 6. Core limitations

- Public benchmark, not operator-specific aircraft inspection data.
- Small holdout sizes and influential tail errors.
- M6 95% bootstrap interval crosses zero.
- OOD robustness remains unresolved.
- M8 is internal blind re-splitting, not independent external validation.
- Continuous challenger is generic; no canonical NS-MDS attribution.
- No certification, regulatory approval or airline endorsement is implied.
