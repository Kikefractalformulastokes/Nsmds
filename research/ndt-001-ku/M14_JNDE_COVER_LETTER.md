# Cover Letter — Journal of Nondestructive Evaluation

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Evidence-First Assurance of Ultrasonic Guided-Wave Damage Localization: Blind Re-evaluation, Tail Risk, Distribution Shift, and Claim Control,”** for publication in the *Journal of Nondestructive Evaluation*.

Data-driven NDE studies are commonly summarized through aggregate benchmark accuracy. In safety-relevant inspection, however, an attractive mean error can coexist with unstable effects, rare large failures, sensitivity to evaluation partition, and deterioration under distribution shift. This manuscript studies that gap using a public ultrasonic guided-wave delamination-localization dataset and a fully pinned computational evidence chain.

On the original noise-free holdout, a generic two-neighbour continuous localization method reduced observed mean absolute error from 15.000 mm to 8.668 mm. A stricter assurance analysis materially changed the interpretation: the paired bootstrap 95% interval for improvement crossed zero, maximum localization error exceeded 100 mm, the apparent effect contracted to 3.98% under a deterministic internal blind re-evaluation, and the continuous method underperformed the nearest-neighbour reference under the studied mixed-noise out-of-distribution condition. Ablation further showed that the observed improvement is attributable to generic interpolation rather than a proprietary mechanism.

We believe the manuscript is relevant to the journal because its contribution is specifically about nondestructive-evaluation evidence quality: reproducible provenance, frozen benchmarking, paired uncertainty, tail-risk reporting, distribution-shift stress testing, ablation, blind re-evaluation, and explicit control of the claims that benchmark evidence can support.

The manuscript does not claim aircraft operational validation, certification or regulatory approval, independent external validation, or performance attributable to canonical NS-MDS. These limitations are stated explicitly.

The source dataset is publicly available through KU Leuven RDR (DOI 10.48804/JAIG58), and the computational evidence chain has been hash-pinned and reproduced in a successful end-to-end workflow. Code and reproducibility materials can be archived/released in accordance with the journal's review policy.

We confirm that the manuscript will be submitted as original work, is not under consideration elsewhere at the time of submission, and that all authors will approve the final submitted version.
