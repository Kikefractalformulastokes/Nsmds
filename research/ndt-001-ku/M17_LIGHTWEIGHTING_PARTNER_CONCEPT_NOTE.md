# M17 — Partner Concept Note

## Evidence-Assured Ultrasonic Inspection for Lightweight Composite Structures

### Call
Transnational Eureka Lightweighting Call 2026
International deadline: 8 October 2026, 17:00 CEST
Spanish national application deadline: 15 October 2026

### Problem
Ultrasonic and guided-wave damage-localisation models can show strong average benchmark performance while still failing under distribution shift, noise, sparse spatial sampling or rare tail cases. For lightweight composite structures, those hidden failure modes create a barrier between promising algorithms and trustworthy industrial deployment.

### Proposed solution
Develop and experimentally validate an evidence-assurance layer for ultrasonic/guided-wave inspection of lightweight composite structures. The project will combine physical test specimens and ultrasonic acquisition with frozen benchmarking, continuous localisation, uncertainty/OOD detection, tail-risk analysis, blinded re-evaluation and auditable acceptance gates.

### CoreSyn contribution
- assurance architecture and frozen benchmark governance;
- reproducible evaluation pipeline;
- localisation and baseline comparison framework;
- uncertainty/OOD and tail-risk analysis;
- blinded validation protocol;
- evidence pack and deployment acceptance criteria;
- software integration and commercialisation pathway.

### Partner sought
An eligible organisation from another participating Eureka country with one or more of:
- ultrasonic/NDT instrumentation and acquisition capability;
- guided-wave or structural-health-monitoring expertise;
- lightweight composite specimen design/manufacturing;
- aerospace/composite industrial validation capability;
- experimental test facilities for controlled defects and blinded trials.

### Proposed partner contribution
- define industrial composite use case;
- manufacture/provide representative specimens or structures;
- generate independent physical ultrasonic/guided-wave datasets;
- execute blinded test campaign;
- contribute industrial requirements and acceptance criteria;
- support exploitation and route to market.

### Indicative work packages
WP1 Requirements, specimens and frozen protocol
WP2 Physical NDT acquisition and controlled damage campaign
WP3 Assurance/localisation software integration
WP4 Noise, OOD and failure-mode stress testing
WP5 Independent blinded validation and statistical analysis
WP6 Evidence pack, industrial acceptance criteria and exploitation

### Current evidence base
CoreSyn has completed a reproducible assurance study on a public simulated guided-wave localisation dataset. The study showed that apparent benchmark improvement can materially weaken under bootstrap uncertainty, distribution shift, tail-risk inspection and blinded re-evaluation. This result motivates — rather than substitutes for — the proposed independent physical validation.

### Claims deliberately excluded
The existing benchmark is not presented as aircraft validation, certification evidence, airline approval, external experimental validation or proof that canonical NS-MDS caused the observed improvement.

### Commercial objective
Deliver a marketable assurance layer and validation methodology that helps NDT/SHM technology providers and lightweight-structure manufacturers determine when model-based inspection is sufficiently evidenced for a defined operational use case.

### Desired consortium shape
CoreSyn/Spanish innovative SME + one strong industrial/technology partner in another eligible country, with an optional university/research laboratory as experimental subcontractor or additional partner where national rules permit.
