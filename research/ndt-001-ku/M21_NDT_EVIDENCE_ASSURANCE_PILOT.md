# M21 — CoreSyn NDT Evidence Assurance Pilot

## Positioning
CoreSyn provides an independent evidence-assurance layer around existing NDT and structural-health-monitoring workflows. It does not replace certified NDT personnel, approved inspection procedures, OEM instructions, Level III authority, maintenance organizations, or regulatory approval.

## Buyer problem
An inspection or localization model can look strong on an average benchmark while still failing under blind re-evaluation, distribution shift, noise, or tail cases. CoreSyn tests those failure boundaries before a customer relies on the system operationally.

## Pilot offer
**NDT Evidence Assurance Pilot**

Input:
- customer-provided historical or validation dataset;
- existing algorithm/model/output, where available;
- agreed operational questions and acceptance criteria.

CoreSyn work:
1. Evidence/provenance audit.
2. Reproducible baseline reconstruction.
3. Blind or held-out re-evaluation.
4. Noise and distribution-shift stress tests where scientifically appropriate.
5. Tail-risk and failure-case analysis.
6. Ablation / attribution tests where feasible.
7. Claim-control review: what the evidence supports and what it does not.
8. Reproducibility manifest and evidence pack.

Deliverables:
- executive assurance report;
- technical evidence pack;
- reproducible benchmark record;
- failure-mode register;
- PASS / CONDITIONAL / HOLD / FAIL assessment against pre-agreed evidence gates;
- roadmap for any additional validation required.

## Scientific proof asset
CoreSyn has completed a reproducible ultrasonic guided-wave assurance case study on a public composite-damage localization dataset. The study demonstrates the assurance workflow rather than claiming certification, airline validation, aircraft validation, or canonical NS-MDS performance.

Observed case-study findings include a substantial mean improvement for generic continuous interpolation on the primary holdout, but the paired bootstrap interval did not establish conventional 95% superiority and performance reversed under one mixed-noise OOD condition. This is precisely the commercial point: average benchmark improvement alone is not enough evidence for trust.

## Core message
**We find where an inspection/model appears to work, where it stops working, and what evidence is still missing before a high-risk decision should rely on it.**
