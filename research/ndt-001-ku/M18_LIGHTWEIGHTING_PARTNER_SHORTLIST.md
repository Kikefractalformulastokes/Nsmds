## Call mechanics relevant to shortlist
Participating funded countries include Austria, Belgium, Estonia, Germany, Lithuania, Poland, Slovakia, Spain, South Africa, South Korea and Türkiye. Consortium requires at least two independent legal entities from at least two participating countries. No organisation or country may exceed 70% of project budget. Maximum project duration is 36 months.

Spain: minimum budget EUR 175,000 per company; startups/small companies may receive up to 85% of eligible costs. Spanish international R&D funding application is due 15 October 2026 after the 8 October Eureka deadline.

Austria: mobility-sector lightweighting is explicitly eligible; startups/small businesses can receive up to 80% and research organisations/universities up to 85%, subject to national rules.
