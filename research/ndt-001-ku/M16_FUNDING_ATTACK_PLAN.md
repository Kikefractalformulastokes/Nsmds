# M16 — Funding Attack Plan

## Objective
Fund the next industrial validation phase of CoreSyn/NDT without retroactively altering the funding declaration of the current JNDE paper.

## Priority 1 — EIC Accelerator 2026
**Target batch:** 4 November 2026.

Why not 2 September: EIC recommends submitting the short proposal roughly 10 weeks before the intended full-proposal batch. Unless an approved short proposal already exists, 2 September is not a credible full-proposal target.

### Positioning
CoreSyn should not be pitched as a consultancy. Position as an evidence and model-assurance technology platform for high-risk scientific and engineering systems, with NDT/composite inspection as a validation vertical.

### EIC package to build
1. Short proposal narrative.
2. Pitch deck.
3. 3-minute video script.
4. Technology differentiation and IP position.
5. TRL/current evidence table.
6. Market and beachhead segmentation.
7. Business model and pricing.
8. Commercial traction/evidence pipeline.
9. Team and capability gaps.
10. 18–24 month workplan and use of funds.
11. Risk register and regulatory/assurance pathway.
12. Milestone map from public benchmark to independent industrial validation.

### Core scientific guardrail
Do not present the KU/Bristol benchmark as aircraft validation or canonical NS-MDS validation. Use it as evidence of CoreSyn's assurance methodology and failure-detection discipline.

## Priority 2 — Eureka Lightweighting Call 2026
**International deadline:** 8 October 2026, 17:00 CEST.
**Spanish CDTI follow-on application:** 15 October 2026.

### Spain funding facts from current call
- Spanish startups/small companies: up to 85% of eligible R&D costs.
- Minimum budget per Spanish company: EUR 175,000.
- Universities/research organisations may participate as subcontractors under the Spanish rules.

### Best project concept
**Evidence-Assured Ultrasonic Inspection for Lightweight Composite Structures**

Proposed R&D objective: develop and independently validate an assurance layer for ultrasonic/guided-wave damage localization in lightweight composite structures, with explicit OOD detection, tail-risk control, reproducibility, uncertainty and industrial acceptance criteria.

### Consortium profile
Minimum useful structure:
- Spain: CoreSyn / Spanish innovative company — assurance engine, benchmark governance, model evidence, software integration.
- Second eligible country: composite/NDT technology company, aerospace supplier, sensor/ultrasound company or research-performing SME.
- Optional technical subcontractor: university/research lab for specimens, guided-wave testing and independent experimental campaign.

### Partner search targets
Priority partner classes:
1. composite aerospace Tier-1/Tier-2 suppliers;
2. ultrasonic/NDT instrumentation SMEs;
3. SHM/guided-wave technology companies;
4. lightweight composite manufacturers;
5. applied university labs able to run blinded physical validation.

## Priority 3 — Eurostars Call 11
**Deadline:** 10 September 2026, 14:00 CET.

### Eligibility constraints
- innovative SME must lead;
- at least two independent entities;
- at least two Eurostars countries;
- at least one partner from an EU Member State or Horizon Europe Associated Country;
- SMEs from Eurostars countries must account for at least 50% of total cost excluding subcontracting;
- no participant/country may exceed 70% of budget;
- duration <=36 months;
- civil applications only.

### Spain funding
Current Eurostars information indicates small Spanish companies can receive up to 60% of eligible project costs, potentially higher subject to national budget; CDTI documentation also notes a general ceiling of 70% and a maximum of EUR 400,000 per beneficiary for Eurostars-3 national funding rounds.

### Decision
Treat 10 September as an opportunistic sprint only if a strong international partner can be secured immediately. Do not submit a weak consortium merely to meet the date. Otherwise use the March 2027 Eurostars call as the robust target.
